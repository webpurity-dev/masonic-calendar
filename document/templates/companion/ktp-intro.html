Introduction to Knight Templar Priests

The Holy Royal Arch Knight Templar Priests and Order of Holy Wisdom, is a gentle, reflective Religious Order.

The Order is Worldwide and is now administered from it’s headquarters in York, with more than 260 Tabernacles in over 20 Countries, from Australia to South Africa and Spain to Brazil.

In England there are 24 Districts, comprising of 193 Tabernacles. There are Two Tabernacles Meeting in Dorset (Blandford & Kinson) and One in Marlborough Wiltshire, forming the District of Dorset & Wiltshire. Locations of all our Tabernacles can be found on our website: knighttemplarpriests.com.

To qualify for Membership as a Knight Templar Priest a Candidate must be a subscribing Master Mason of 3 Years in the Craft, a subscribing Royal Arch Mason, a subscribing Knight Templar Mason and must make a Statement of Faith. Why being a Christian is necessary, is found in the Admission Ceremony into this beautiful Masonic Order.
