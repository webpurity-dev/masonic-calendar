Lodges associated with the Province of Dorset

London Dorset Lodge 3221

Warrant dated 14 February 1907. Centenary Warrant granted 2007. Meet at Freemasons' Hall, Great Queen Street, London on the first Monday in March (Installation), June, November & December.

Any Lodge in the Province of Dorset may become associated to this Lodge on payment of a ‘one off’ fee equal to 20% of the annual subscription in force at the time of the application. Thereafter any member of such Lodge may attend any meeting and dinner of the Lodge on payment of the dining fee.

Old Shirburnian Lodge 3304

Warrant dated 10 April 1908. Consecrated 28 May 1908. Centenary Warrant granted 2008. Meet at Freemasons' Hall, Great Queen Street, London on the third Tuesday in February, 5.00pm (Installation); third Saturday in June, 11.15am at Sherborne by Dispensation; and on the fourth Tuesday in October (London) 5.30pm.

The lodge is established primarily for the benefit of all those educated at, and connected with Sherborne School, viz:
(a) Old Shirburnians
(b) Members of the Old Shirburnian Society
(c) Masters of Sherborne School
(d) Past and Present Governors of Sherborne School
