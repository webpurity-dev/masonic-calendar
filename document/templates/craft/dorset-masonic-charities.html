(But I am too proud and embarrassed to ask)

We in the Masonic Province of Dorset have unique access to two wonderful Masonic Charities able to provide assistance to those in need. Both Charities have very similar objectives and criteria, and both are run for the benefit of Dorset Freemasons (subscribing or un-attached) and Masonic Widows.

Dorset Masonic Care

Registered Charity 217655

Dorset Ashlar Housing Trust

Registered Charity 900151

Founded in 1880, providing relief & assistance to those in need of financial support. More than 80% of applications are dealt with within 48 hours, and all are handled discretely and in strict confidence. For more complex needs DMC works together with the Freemasons' Charity (formerly MCF).

Owns Kenneth Cross Court containing 10x2 bedroom flats and 2x1 bedroom flats in a purpose built complex in Parkstone. Accommodation is provided at heavily reduced monthly rents and residents are assured of friendly and regular contact.
