The Freemasons’ Charity, formerly the Masonic Charitable Foundation, supports a wide range of causes, combining financial aid, volunteer work, and community engagement to improve lives.

It is a funded entirely by Freemasons and it aims to build better lives by promoting opportunity, independence, and wellbeing. The organization is governed under the United Grand Lodge of England (UGLE).

  • It provides grants to individuals in need, including support for the elderly, sick, disabled, and families facing hardship.
  • It provides grants to charities both local and national.
  • In Dorset for the financial year ending 1st April 2025, five local charities received grants totalling £23,250 and 35 individuals were helped with grants of £131,815.
  • Figures are not yet published for 2026, but we know as a Province that we have supported MyTime Young Carers with a Community Grant of £50,000 and Lewis Manning Trust with a Grant of £40,000.

For further information visit https://freemasonscharity.org.uk
